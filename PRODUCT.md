# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using Brave or another Chromium browser to find tracks inside large [untitled] music projects. They are usually listening, comparing, or organizing work-in-progress music and need a known title immediately.

## Product Purpose

Correct [untitled]'s in-project search ordering so an exact song title is always the first result, without replacing the native player or requiring users to move their library elsewhere. Success means the intended track is visible immediately after typing its title.

## Brand Personality

Invisible, fast, trustworthy. The extension should feel like [untitled] itself simply became more accurate.

## Anti-references

No floating extension panel, separate search modal, decorative branding, ads, analytics, or unfamiliar controls. Do not turn a ranking correction into another interface to learn.

## Design Principles

- Exact intent wins over fuzzy similarity.
- Preserve the native [untitled] interaction model.
- Do all processing locally and request the narrowest possible access.
- Keep tie-breaking stable so results do not jump unpredictably.
- Fail quietly when [untitled] changes rather than breaking the page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve Untitled's existing semantic rows, focus behavior, labels, contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. Do not recreate interactive controls or rely on color to communicate ranking.
