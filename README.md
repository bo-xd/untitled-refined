# Untitled Refined

Search for the song you meant. Get it first.

Untitled Refined fixes project search on [untitled](https://untitled.stream) and adds a little polish without changing the site's design.

## What gets better

**Search**
- Exact song titles appear first, even when apostrophes, punctuation, or capitalization differ.
- Add `v2` or `feat` to a search to filter by version or features. A result count shows while you search.
- Use **↑ / ↓** to highlight a result and **Enter** to play it.

**Favorites**
- Tap the heart on a track or in the player. Favorites are pinned to the top of their project, win close search matches, and **Favorites only** shows just them.
- The heart button in the bottom-left corner (**Shift+F**) lists favorites from every project, plus recently played tracks. Click one to jump to it and play it.
- Copy your favorites as text, or back them up to a file and restore them.

**Listening**
- Play counts: a track counts as played after 30 seconds. **Most played** sorts a project by them.
- **Group versions** folds `[V1]`, `[V2]`, … into one row showing the newest version.
- After a reload, you're offered to resume the last track where you left off.
- Press **.** or click the player title to jump to the track that's playing.

**Shortcuts** (Untitled's own Space, ←/→ and ↑/↓ still work)

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **L** | Favorite the playing track |
| **Shift+N / Shift+P** | Next / previous track |
| **J / K** | Move through the list, **Enter** to play |
| **.** | Jump to the playing track |
| **Shift+F** | Open the favorites panel |

Everything runs locally in the page: no tracking, data collection, network requests, or extra browser permissions. Favorites, play counts, and settings stay in this browser’s local storage for untitled.stream.

To install, download and extract the repo, open your browser's extensions page, enable **Developer mode**, and choose **Load unpacked**.
